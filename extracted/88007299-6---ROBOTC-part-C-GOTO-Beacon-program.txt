What does the GOTO Beacon program do?
Once the program is downloaded to the controller, the robot will spin in place and will look for the red beacon. If the red beacon is in sight, the robot will move towards it, otherwise it continues to spin.
It is a head start for your job of programming the robot.

Introduction to Engineering – E10
ReadPD function
This function reads the intensity of all 8 Infrared Photo detectors and stores
the intensity (0 to 1023) into PD0-PD7 variables. It also produces the sum
of all 8 readings (PD_sum). The sensing sensitivity is set by expose_time.

LEDs- Light Emitting Diodes: 
   A special kind of diode: They allow current to      flow in one direction.
The 5 LEDs on the IRB are for status indication only.  They don’t serve any purpose for the operation of the board.
LED 1-3, show which IR detector is the active detector, blinking. 
When LED 4 is on, the circuit is at the low sensitivity setting.
LED 5 indicates the tuning frequency, when on, robot is looking for the red beacon
