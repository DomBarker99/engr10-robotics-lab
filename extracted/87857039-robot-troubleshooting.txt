The Robot is not Responding at all
Check the IR Board (IRB) using the test board: all five red led lights must be on. The 5 LEDs on the IRB are for status indication only.  They don’t serve any purpose for the operation of the board
LED1 to LED3 show which IR detector is the active detector. In normal operation, however, the VEX controller scans these detectors at a high rate which gives the appearance of random flashing. LED2 and 3 appear to be on because they are blinking very fast   
LED1 to 3
LED 4 indicated the low/high sensitivity setting.  When LED 4 is on, the circuit is at the low sensitivity setting.  When it is off, the circuit is at the high sensitivity setting.
LED4
LED 5 indicates the tuning frequency.  When LED 4 is on, 1 kHz is selected, the robot is looking for red beacon.  When it is off, 10 kHz is selected, the robot is looking for the green beacon.

The robot spins but does not see the red beacon
Move the beacon closer to the robot, if it sees the beacon then the battery in the portable beacon or the one on the robot is running down
The robot locates the red beacon but it avoids it
The ambient light needs to be adjusted: it is originally set at 200
The motors are connected to the wrong ports, switch the ports. Or wire extensions are connected wrong (black to black, red to red)
The robot wonders too much while looking for the beacon
Some of the PDs are not receiving the signal
Make sure all 8 PDs are somewhat horizontal and not looking up or being blocked.

The IRB was fine before, but it is dead now.
Everything seems to work fine: IRB, motors are new, sensors are fine, connections are right, battery is fully charged, so what is wrong?
It could be the controller, get another one from your lab instruction
Check the wires for damage.
Check the fuse, the board could have been shorted accidently
Get a working board from your instructor
The arm is not working, the motor is too weak to lift the arm
As a last resource, use a counter weight at the back end of the arm, not the best solution since it increases the weight of the motor
The best way is to fix this is to increase the torque applied to the arm. Attach a small gear to the motor shaft that meshes with a larger gear that is attached to the arm.
